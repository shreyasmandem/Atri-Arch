Your practice's site, with the studio inside it.

Most clients arrive with a plot, a budget and no drawing. The widget puts the design engine on your own page: six questions, and they leave with a real scheme — plan, cost and Vastu report — before anyone has sat down.

Try it. The button in the corner is the widget, running on this page exactly as it would on yours. Everything it produces comes from the same committee that sits in the studio.

What this page proves

  • Isolation. This host page forces a serif face and a 14 px radius on every button and input. None of it reaches the widget: it renders inside a shadow root, so their CSS cannot break it and it cannot break their site.
  • Their brand, not ours. Accent, practice name and button label come from the script tag. The widget is the practice's tool.
  • No build. One tag, no npm, no bundle. A webmaster pastes it and it works.

Installation is one tag

<script src="https://your-aip-host/embed/aip-widget.js" data-key="aip_pk_your_public_key" data-practice="Kalpa Studio" data-accent="#7FA3D4" data-label="Design my home" defer></script>

The public key is origin-bound server-side, so it is safe in page source. Secret keys never leave your server.
