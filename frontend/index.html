From a sentence to a set of drawings, in about a minute

  1. 01Say it your way

    Plot size in feet or metres, road side, floors, rooms - and anything you'd love. The studio asks only for what you left out.

  2. 02Three schemes, drawn live

    Living in front, a dining hub at the heart, the stair stacked floor to floor, every bedroom by its bath. Three real schemes, drawn as the studio works them out.

  3. 03Thirteen specialists argue

    The engineer, the architects and their specialists - code, structure, daylight, airflow, Vastu, cost - argue over every scheme, then pick one and say why.

  4. 04Explore, then change it

    Walk the 3D house, switch facades and colours, watch the breeze move through it - then change anything: tap a suggestion or say it, and the studio redraws.
